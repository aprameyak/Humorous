# Privacy Policy for Humorous

**Last updated:** December 2024

## Introduction

Humorous ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Email Address**: Used for account creation and authentication
- **User ID**: Generated by our authentication system for account management

### Usage Data
- **Joke Reactions**: Your like/dislike responses to jokes to personalize your experience
- **Humor Preferences**: Your preferred joke styles to improve content recommendations
- **App Usage**: Basic analytics to improve app functionality

### Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **App Performance**: Crash reports and performance metrics
- **Network Information**: Connection type for troubleshooting

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Account Management**: Create and manage your user account
- **Content Personalization**: Tailor joke recommendations based on your preferences
- **App Functionality**: Provide core app features and improve user experience
- **Technical Support**: Troubleshoot issues and improve app performance

### Analytics
We use anonymous, aggregated data to:
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Understand user behavior patterns
- Enhance content recommendations
- Fix bugs and technical issues

## Data Storage and Security

### Data Storage
- **Supabase**: User accounts and preferences are stored securely
- **Local Device**: Some data is cached locally for offline functionality
- **Third-party APIs**: Joke content is fetched from external services

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Authentication**: Secure user authentication via Supabase
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to user data by authorized personnel only

## Third-Party Services

### Services We Use
- **Supabase**: Authentication and database services
- **Google Gemini API**: AI-powered joke generation
- **External Joke APIs**: Additional joke content sources

### Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:
- As required by law
- To protect our rights and safety
- With your explicit consent

## Your Rights

### Access and Control
- **View Data**: Request access to your personal information
- **Update Information**: Modify your account details
- **Delete Account**: Request account deletion and data removal
- **Opt-out**: Disable analytics and personalization features

### Data Retention
- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active
- **Usage Data**: Automatically deleted after 6 months
- **Analytics**: Aggregated and anonymized after 12 months

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Sending you an email notification
- Updating the "Last updated" date
